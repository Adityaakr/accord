# Request early access.

Accord is pre-launch and early access is by request. Join as a token project, a market maker, an integration partner, a vendor or a buyer. In a deal room your position stays sealed; the other side sees only the agreed terms.

## How you join.

- 01 Seal your mandate Each company sets its limits in the browser and seals them. Only the sealed mandate and its commitment hash go on NEAR; the other side never sees the numbers.

- 02 Negotiate, with or without an agent The Sealed Table negotiates the terms. If you have no agent, a person on your side negotiates through the application against the other side's agent.

- 03 Both sides approve Both companies approve the terms before they take effect. Visible to both: the approved terms, the schedule, every payment, exception and refusal.
