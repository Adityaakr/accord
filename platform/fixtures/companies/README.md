# Fictional companies for the demo

Northwind Foundation (a token treasury, the buyer) and Halcyon Markets (a market maker, the vendor) are fictional. Every amount is in USDC base units (6 decimals): `"12000000"` is `12.00 USDC`. The amounts are small because the live demo uses Circle's testnet USDC from its faucet.

- `procurement.*.json`: a buyer ceiling of `40` and target of `32` against a vendor floor of `30` and target of `38`, so a deal near `36` in three milestones of `12` is expected, and a walk-away is possible if the negotiators misjudge.
- `market-making.*.json`: a retainer of `5` to `8` per six-minute demo period, six slots, threshold four, on Rhea's USDC/wNEAR DCL pool on mainnet (read-only). The passing agreement's conditions come from `probe-venue` on 10 Nov 2026 (spreads `169` to `187 bps`; within `300 bps` about `36 USDC` of bid and `45 USDC` of ask depth, while within `200 bps` the bid side was `0.00` at three of five samples): the buyer pays for at most `220 bps` and at least `20 USDC` on both sides within at most `300 bps`, and the vendor commits to no tighter than `200 bps`, no more than `25 USDC` and a band of at least `300 bps`, so any terms they agree on are ones the pool met at every sample (the 3 Nov conditions, `146 bps` and `32.78 USDC` within `200 bps`, no longer hold). The buyer's `shared` names the schedule (periods, period length, six slots), because the vendor cannot see the buyer's limits and otherwise quoted one slot with a threshold of five, which the structure check refuses (`a50.accord-v2.testnet`, 10 Nov 2026); the stricter agreement (for the `Withheld` period) pairs `market-making.buyer.strict.json` (`max_spread_bps: 30`) with `market-making.vendor.strict.json` (`tightest_spread_bps: 25`, a vendor that overpromises), so the two can agree on a spread a `0.2%` fee pool cannot meet.
- `try.*.json`: the public `/try` room's pair: twelve items of `0.01 USDC` (`120000` base units in all), due an hour apart, over seven days. Both sides accept exactly those terms, so the re-arm can open a new room without anyone (`ARCHITECTURE.md` section 11).
- `halcyon/accord.json`: the domain file, in the list form: the hosted demo's recipient and authority, and the `/try` room's. Serve it at `https://<DEMO_VENDOR_DOMAIN>/.well-known/accord.json`; the real account names from `make setup` replace these.
- Mandate shape and the within-limits rules: `INTERFACES.md` section 6.
